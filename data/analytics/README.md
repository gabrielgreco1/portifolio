`src/data/visitor-history.json` is generated by `scripts/import-visitor-google.py`. Supply a directory with the public [GeoNames cities500.zip and countryInfo.txt](https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/) downloads. The public gazetteer is downloaded in bulk; visitor records are never sent to a geocoder. The exact downloaded zip SHA-256 is retained in the JSON.

City names are matched within their ISO country, accent-insensitively. Exact current names take priority over aliases. A match is used only when there is one candidate at that priority. There is no popularity-based choice, nearest-neighbor guess or fallback to a country center. Coordinates are rounded to 0.1° and describe city centers, not visitor locations. This finite gazetteer does not prove complete coverage of every place with the same name. Remaining ambiguous, missing and unknown cities remain searchable with their actual counts and no marker. Current coverage: 263 matched, 44 ambiguous, 4 unmatched, 9 unknown. A future source with region/city IDs can improve disambiguation.

GeoNames data is licensed **CC BY 4.0**, attributed in the map. See its [schema and license](https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/readme.txt). Historical metrics remain separate from the portfolio's live 90-second presence and 30-minute visit rules. This snapshot intentionally does not create session IDs or first/last visit timestamps from aggregated counts.
