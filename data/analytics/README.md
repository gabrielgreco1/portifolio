Every named city joined one-to-one to the city/country table, and its active-user and engaged-session counts were identical. Only these 311 verified city/region associations are retained in `ga4-city-regions-2026-10-08.psv` (one region followed by its cities on each line). Its browser-capture FNV-1a is `0d0ac427`; the generated JSON retains its SHA-256. Unknown-city rows are not joined across reports because their country is ambiguous. The original 320 records and reported totals remain unchanged.

Names match within country and, where identified, the Google region. Region names are matched to pinned GeoNames administrative codes with a limited country-specific handling of labels such as “State of” in Brazil and “District” in Portugal. A region label that cannot be matched reliably does not filter candidates: Maputo city, for example, is not forced into the similarly named province. Accents and hyphen/space differences are normalized; exact current city names take priority over aliases. Two reviewed spelling/renaming aliases include their official sources in `city-aliases.json`: Embu → Embu das Artes (São Paulo state law) and Charneca da Caparica → Charneca de Caparica (Almada municipal document).

A marker requires one remaining candidate, or agreement of all remaining candidates on the same rounded 0.1° center. Pinhais has two gazetteer entries at that precision, so both GeoNames IDs are retained. There is no population-based choice or fallback to a country center. Current coverage: **305 matched, five ambiguous, one unmatched, nine unknown**. The six named unresolved cities are Estrela, José Bonifácio, Mountain View, São José, São Miguel and Vicente de Carvalho. All remain searchable with their original counts and region, without an invented marker. This finite gazetteer does not prove completeness of every place with the same name; coordinates represent approximate city centers, not personal locations.
